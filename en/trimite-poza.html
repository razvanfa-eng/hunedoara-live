Send a photo

Many places in the guide still lack a good photo. If you took the picture yourself, we can publish it on gohd.ro with your name as the author.

The image must be JPG or PNG, preferably landscape and at least 1600 px wide, up to 8 MB. We do not publish photos of identifiable people without their consent, nor photos taken by somebody else.

Your details (name and e-mail) are used only to credit you and, if needed, to contact you about the photo; your e-mail is not published or shared. The author name and the photo will be public on gohd.ro. You can ask for the photo or your data to be removed at any time by writing to hunedoaragohd@gmail.com.
