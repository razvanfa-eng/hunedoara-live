A weekend in the Jiu Valley

Mining heritage, a forest that changes colour and a themed trail through tunnels — two days in the towns at the foot of the Parâng mountains.

2 days · 7 stops

The Jiu Valley is best understood through its mining past. Day one links the museum in Petroșani with the former mine in Petrila and the scenery nearby; day two heads outdoors, on an easy trail near Vulcan or, in winter, to the ski slopes.

Day 1 — Mining and caves

  1. Mining Museum, Petroșani Museum · Petroșani

    The only museum in Romania dedicated exclusively to coal-mining technology.

  2. Petrila Mine (Planeta Petrila) Historic industrial site · Petrila

    The oldest mine in the Jiu Valley, now turned into an open-air museum.

  3. Two-Coloured Forest optional Natural phenomenon · Petrila

    A hillside that looks cut in two colours, at the Tăia Gorges near Petrila — evergreen spruce on one side, golden beech on the other.

    Best in autumn, while the beech trees change colour (see the place's page).

  4. Bolii Cave optional Cave · Petrila

    A 455 m river cave with electric lighting, on the edge of the Grădiștea Muncelului-Cioclovina Natural Park.

    Length: 455 m

Day 2 — Outdoors

  1. Tunnels Trail Themed trail · Vulcan

    An easy-to-moderate themed trail near Vulcan, leading to the Devil's Cave and Waterfall.

    Length: ~6 km (3–4 hours round trip)

  2. Straja ski resort optional Skiing · Vâlcan Mountains

    Declared a resort in 2002, with the county's largest ski area — about 26 km of slopes.

    In winter: skiing on the Straja slopes.

  3. Parâng ski slopes optional Skiing · Parâng Mountains

    A ski area about 15 km from Petroșani, with a chairlift climbing from 950 to 1,650 m.

    In winter: an alternative for skiing, at Parâng.

Where to eat or stay

  • Atrium Hotel Hotel & restaurant · Vulcan
  • Complex Keops Restaurant · Petroșani
