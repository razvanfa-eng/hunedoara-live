Zarand Country: gold, history and thermal waters

The Gold Museum in Brad, Crișan's house, Țebea and Vața Spa, around Brad and Baia de Criș.

2 days · 7 stops

Brad and its surroundings link three themes: the gold of the Metaliferi Mountains, the uprisings of 1784 and 1848, and the villages on the Crișul Alb. Day one stays in Brad and Ribița commune; day two heads to Țebea, Grohot and Vața de Jos Spa.

Day 1 — Gold and uprising

  1. Gold Museum, Brad Museum · Brad

    The largest collection of native gold in Europe, with pieces untouched by any jeweller.

  2. Crișan Memorial House Memorial house · Brad

    The reconstructed house of Gheorghe Crișan, one of the leaders of the 1784–1785 peasant uprising.

  3. Roman Steps (Ruda-Brad) optional Archaeological mining site · Brad

    One of the few Roman-era mining galleries preserved in the Apuseni Mountains, dug nearly 2,000 years ago.

    Gallery length: ~200 m

Day 2 — Țebea, Grohot and Vața

  1. Moților Pantheon, Țebea Memorial complex · Zarand Country

    The heroes' cemetery in Țebea, with Avram Iancu's grave and the famous Horea's Oak.

  2. Grohot Natural Bridge optional Natural monument · Zarand Country

    A natural monument in the Uibărești valley, about 15 km from Țebea, protected for its natural bridge and limestone vegetation.

  3. Vața de Jos thermal baths (Vața Băi) Spa resort · Vața de Jos

    A thermal spa with 36–38 °C waters in the Crișul Alb valley, reopened after more than a decade of neglect.

  4. Wooden Church, Ciungani optional Wooden church · Vața de Jos

    A wooden church from around 1600, with the tallest tower of any wooden church in Hunedoara county — 14 m.
