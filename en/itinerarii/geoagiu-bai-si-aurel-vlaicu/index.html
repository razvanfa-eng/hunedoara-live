Geoagiu-Băi and Aurel Vlaicu's village

The spa resort, the Germisara Roman baths, the rotunda and fort at Geoagiu and the Aurel Vlaicu memorial house, in a day around Geoagiu.

one day · 6 stops

A full day around Geoagiu: thermal water and Roman remains in the morning, the village of the inventor Aurel Vlaicu and the church in Boz in the afternoon.

Day 1 — Geoagiu

  1. Geoagiu-Băi spa resort Spa resort · Geoagiu

    A year-round spa resort with thermal waters, an outdoor pool complex with an Olympic pool, and Roman baths almost two millennia old.

  2. Germisara Roman Baths Roman archaeological site · Geoagiu

    The Roman baths of Geoagiu-Băi, with pools cut into the rock, where gold offerings were made to the deities of the warm springs.

  3. Germisara Roman Fort (Cigmău) optional Roman fort · Geoagiu

    A Roman auxiliary fort near Geoagiu, known locally as the “Giant's Fortress”, with a civilian settlement beside it.

  4. Geoagiu Rotunda Historic monument · Geoagiu

    A circular Romanesque chapel, probably the oldest medieval church on Romanian territory.

  5. “Aurel Vlaicu” Memorial Complex Memorial house · Aurel Vlaicu

    The birthplace and museum of inventor and aviator Aurel Vlaicu, in the village near Orăștie that now bears his name.

  6. Clocota Waterfall optional Waterfall · Geoagiu

    A small waterfall on the Clocota stream, close to the centre of Geoagiu-Băi resort.

Where to eat or stay

  • Hotel Germisara Resort & SPA Hotel & restaurant · Geoagiu
  • Bistro Merinde Restaurant · Orăștie
