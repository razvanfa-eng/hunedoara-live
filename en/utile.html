Useful information

Transport, contacts and practical links for visiting Hunedoara county.

Transport

  • By car: DN7 (Deva–Simeria–Orăștie) and DN66 (Simeria–Petroșani–Valea Jiului).
  • By train: the Deva–Simeria–Petroșani–Lupeni line, plus connections to Arad/Timișoara and Sibiu/București.
  • Nearest airport: Sibiu or Cluj-Napoca (~1.5–2 hours by car).

Emergencies

  • National emergency number (police, fire, ambulance): 112.
  • Salvamont Hunedoara (mountain rescue): 0725 826 668 (national 0-SALVAMONT dispatch) or 0354 407 457 (county dispatch).

Maps & useful links

  • Tourist maps in the Destinations (Waze/Google Maps link on every detail page).
  • Other useful links: Hunedoara County Council, Salvamont Hunedoara (mountain rescue), weather forecast (ANM), Retezat National Park.
