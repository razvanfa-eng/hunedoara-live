Iconic landmarks

Municipality

Deva

The seat of Hunedoara county, overlooked by Deva Citadel on a volcanic hill right in the city centre.

Municipality

Hunedoara

Home to Corvin Castle, one of Europe's largest medieval castles.

Skiing

Straja ski resort

Declared a resort in 2002, with the county's largest ski area — about 26 km of slopes.

UNESCO archaeological site

Sarmizegetusa Regia

The capital of the Dacian kingdom, on a 1,200 m summit — UNESCO World Heritage since 1999.

National park

Retezat National Park

Romania's oldest national park (1935), with over 80 glacial lakes.

Monastery

Prislop Monastery

Founded in 1564, a pilgrimage site linked to Father Arsenie Boca.
